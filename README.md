Entry for the [Dad Joke Showdown](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mfnakmal-dad-joke-showdown-1d2ce3e5)
bounty on make.fun: one dad joke, at most 200 characters, written to
`/output/entry.txt` by `main.py`.

The joke:

> My daughter asked why I keep a ladder in the kitchen. I told her, 'For high tea.'

It was picked from six candidates scored against the judge brief's criteria
in order — a dad joke a father would tell at the table, a pun that lands
cleanly on the first read, fresh framing (a ladder in the kitchen), tight at
81 characters with the punchline at the end, and a rhythm a father could
deliver across a dinner table.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
